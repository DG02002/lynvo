# Policy writing findings

Reviewed July 31, 2026 against the implemented product limits, current Lynvo terminology, pricing copy, and the project writing system in `docs/WRITING.md`.

## Safe clarity and consistency changes

- Define Lynvo, account, Recent Link, Plugin Server, Plugin, Source, credential, and service provider before relying on those terms.
- Replace obsolete or inconsistent terms, including `external worker`, `official Plugin Server`, lowercase product terms, and `resume support`.
- Use `Lynvo Plugin Server` and `Custom Plugin Server` for the two Plugin Server categories.
- Match the cookie interface labels: `Manage cookies`, `Accept all cookies`, and `Reject optional cookies`.
- Replace ambiguous references to “the Service” where `Lynvo` or `Lynvo service` is clearer.
- Use direct, internationally understandable age and account-recovery language.
- Update policy revision dates when the reviewed copy is published.

## Quantitative claim mismatches to correct

- Terms list the 3 MB storage limit and 1 MB per-link limit but omit the maximum of 100 Recent Links.
- Usage policy does not state the implemented Lynvo Plugin Server limits: 15 requests per day and 200 requests per month.
- Policies do not explain that the 200 monthly requests are shared across Lynvo Plugins and Direct media requests.
- Pricing, terms, privacy, and usage policy need the same 90-day default retention and 90-day account-inactivity period.

## Material provisions preserved for legal review

The writing pass must not change these provisions:

- agreement by account creation or use;
- minimum-age and guardian-permission requirements;
- account responsibility and lack of password recovery;
- automatic deletion after 90 days without recorded activity;
- content rights, acceptable-use restrictions, and credential duties;
- suspension, termination, and permanent account deletion rights;
- warranty disclaimers and liability limitations;
- disclosures, international processing, privacy rights, and children’s privacy;
- unilateral service, policy, and terms changes where currently stated.

Clarifying definitions, enforcement language, limits, or data disclosures may affect legal interpretation even when intended only as a writing cleanup. Those edits require human or legal review before publication.
