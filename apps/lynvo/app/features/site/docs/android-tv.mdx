## Prepare your TV and phone

This guide shows you how to open Lynvo on Android TV, sign in from your phone, and configure the recommended video players. You need an Android TV and a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Install these apps before you begin:

- On your TV, install [TV Bro](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phlox.tvwebbrowser)
- On your TV, install [Just (Video) Player](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brouken.player)
- On your TV, install [VLC for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.videolan.vlc)
- On your phone, install the [Google TV app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.videos)

## Open Lynvo in TV Bro

Use the Google TV virtual remote to paste Lynvo’s address instead of typing it with the TV remote:

1. Open [lynvo.app](https://lynvo.app) on your phone
2. Copy `https://lynvo.app`
3. Open **TV Bro** on your Android TV
4. Open the **Google TV** app on your phone
5. Select your TV and open the virtual remote
6. Select the address bar in TV Bro
7. Paste `https://lynvo.app` with the virtual remote keyboard
8. Open the page

Bookmark Lynvo in TV Bro so you can return without entering the address again.

## Sign in with a QR code

Complete authentication on your phone so you do not need to enter account details with the TV remote:

1. Select **Log in** on the Lynvo page displayed on your TV
2. Select **Use a QR code**
3. Scan the QR code with your phone’s camera
4. Open the Lynvo page shown on your phone
5. Sign in to your Lynvo account
6. Approve the TV sign-in if Lynvo asks for confirmation
7. Return to the TV and wait for Lynvo to sign in

If the code expires, request a new QR code on the TV and scan it again.

<DocSection id="play-video">

## Play a video

Lynvo selects a player based on whether the video link supports resume:

- **Just (Video) Player**: the default for links that support resume
- **VLC for Android**: the default for links that do not support resume

Android TV may ask which app should open a video the first time. Select the player Lynvo recommends. You can change the default players later in **Settings**.
