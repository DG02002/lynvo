## Prepare your TV and phone

This guide shows you how to open Lynvo on Android TV, log in from a phone or another device, and configure the recommended video players. Connect the Android TV and the other device to the same Wi-Fi network.

Install these apps before you begin:

- On your TV, install [TV Bro](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phlox.tvwebbrowser)
- On your TV, install [Just (Video) Player](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brouken.player)
- On your TV, install [VLC for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.videolan.vlc)
- On your phone, install the [Google TV app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.videos)

## Open Lynvo in TV Bro

Use the Google TV virtual remote to paste Lynvo’s address instead of typing it with the TV remote:

1. Open [lynvo.dg02002.workers.dev](https://lynvo.dg02002.workers.dev) on your phone
2. Copy `https://lynvo.dg02002.workers.dev`
3. Open **TV Bro** on your Android TV
4. Open the **Google TV** app on your phone
5. Select your TV and open the virtual remote
6. Select the address bar in TV Bro
7. Paste `https://lynvo.dg02002.workers.dev` with the virtual remote keyboard
8. Open the page

If you cannot use the Google TV virtual remote, enter the same address with the TV remote or a connected keyboard.

Bookmark Lynvo in TV Bro so you can return without entering the address again.

## Log in with another device

Complete login on a phone or another device so you do not need to enter account details with the TV remote:

1. Select **Log in** on the Lynvo page displayed on your TV
2. Select **Use a QR code**
3. Choose one method:
   - Scan the QR code with a camera, then open the Lynvo page that appears
   - Open the address displayed with the QR code on another device, then enter the eight-character login code
4. Log in to Lynvo on the other device
5. Approve the TV login if Lynvo asks for confirmation
6. Return to the TV and wait for Lynvo to complete login

If the code expires, select **Generate new code**, then scan the new QR code or enter the new login code.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="remote-play">

## Set up Remote Play

1. Keep Lynvo open in TV Bro on the TV
2. Open Lynvo on the device you want to use as the remote
3. Select **Remote Play**
4. Select the TV from **Available devices**

If the TV does not appear, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network, keep Lynvo open in TV Bro, then select **Search again**.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="play-video">

## Play a video

Lynvo selects a player based on whether the video supports seeking:

- **Just (Video) Player**: the default for videos that support seeking
- **VLC for Android**: the default for videos that require standard playback

Android TV may ask which app should open a video the first time. Select the player Lynvo recommends. You can change the default players later in **Settings**.
