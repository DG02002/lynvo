## Overview

Use TV Bro to open Lynvo on Android TV, then sign in with a QR code from a device where you are already signed in. Lynvo sends selected Playable links to an Android player installed on the TV.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="configure">

## Configure

Install these apps before you begin:

- On Android TV, install [TV Bro](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phlox.tvwebbrowser).
- On Android TV, install [Just (Video) Player](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brouken.player) or [VLC for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.videolan.vlc).
- On an Android phone or tablet, install the [Google TV app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.videos) if you want to use its virtual remote to enter Lynvo's address.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="basics">

## Basics

### Open Lynvo in TV Bro

Use the Google TV virtual remote to paste the address instead of typing it with the Android TV remote:

1. Open `https://lynvo.dg02002.workers.dev` on your phone or tablet and copy the address.
2. Open TV Bro on the Android TV.
3. Open the Google TV app on your phone or tablet and connect its virtual remote to the TV.
4. Select the TV Bro address bar, paste the Lynvo address, and open it.

You can also type the address with the Android TV remote or a connected keyboard. Bookmark Lynvo in TV Bro to return without entering the address again.

### Sign in on the TV

On TV Bro, choose **Sign in** and then **Use a QR code**. Scan the code with a device already signed in to Lynvo, confirm that the activation codes match, and approve the sign-in. Follow the full [QR sign-in steps](/docs/sign-in) if the code expires or approval fails.

### Choose a Library view

Gallery view is the default in TV Bro. It groups related Saved links with Artwork; List view shows each Saved link in a row. Change the selection in [**Settings > General**](/settings/general). Read [Library views](/docs/library-views) for the artwork and show settings.

### Open a video

Choose a Saved link and then a Playable link. Lynvo opens it in the player configured in [**Settings > Player**](/settings/player). Just (Video) Player and VLC are the default players for links with and without HTTP byte-range support. Install your chosen player on the TV before opening a link.

### Send a link from your phone

Keep Lynvo open in TV Bro, then choose the TV in [Remote Play](/docs/remote-play) on another signed-in device. Lynvo sends the selected Playable link to the TV's browser session, which opens it in the configured Android player.

<DocSection id="faq">

## FAQ

<DocsFaq question="Why can’t I type into TV Bro with the remote?">
  Use the Google TV app's virtual remote to paste Lynvo's address. You can also
  use a connected keyboard or enter the address with the TV remote.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Why does the TV not appear in Remote Play?">
  Keep Lynvo open in TV Bro and confirm both devices use the same account. In
  the Remote Play device picker, choose **Search again**.
</DocsFaq>
