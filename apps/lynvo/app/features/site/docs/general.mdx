## Overview

General settings control Lynvo's appearance and how your Library looks and saves links.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="configure">

## Configure

Open [**Settings > General**](/settings/general). Choose an Appearance option, set the automatic-save switch, and select a Saved links view. Changes take effect in Lynvo after you choose them.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="basics">

## Basics

### Choose an appearance

**Appearance** has three options:

- **System** follows your device's theme.
- **Dark** uses the dark theme.
- **Light** uses the light theme.

### Save every playable link automatically

Turn on **Save all links automatically** to save every Playable link Lynvo finds without a selection step. Turn it off to review the results and choose which links belong in the Saved link. With the switch off, refresh actions use **Refresh link choices** when you need to select the new results.

### Choose a Library view

Under **Saved links view**, choose one of the radio cards:

| Option           | What it shows                          | Availability          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| **List view**    | “Browse every saved link in rows.”     | General availability. |
| **Gallery view** | “Group movies and shows with artwork.” | Beta.                 |

TV Bro starts in Gallery view until you change the preference. On other browsers, List view is the default. Read [Library views](/docs/library-views) for date groups, Artwork, and episode settings.

<DocSection id="faq">

## FAQ

<DocsFaq question="Does changing the Library view change my Saved links?">
  No. The setting changes their presentation. It does not add or remove Saved
  links.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Why does TV Bro open in Gallery view?">
  Gallery view is the default for TV Bro. Choose **List view** under **Saved
  links view** to change it.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Can I change automatic saving later?">
  Yes. Turn **Save all links automatically** on or off at any time. With it off,
  choose the links Lynvo found before saving them.
</DocsFaq>

</DocSection>
