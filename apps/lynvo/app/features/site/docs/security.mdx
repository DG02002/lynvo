## Overview

Security and sign-in settings let you end Lynvo sessions on individual devices or delete your account and its stored data.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="configure">

## Configure

Open [**Settings > Security and sign-in**](/settings/security). Choose **Active sessions** to review devices, or **Delete account** to begin permanent deletion.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="basics">

## Basics

### Sign out one device

Open **Active sessions** to see the devices that have accessed your account. The current device is marked **Current session**. Choose **Sign out** beside another device and confirm **Sign out** to end that session. The current session does not have a per-session sign-out button; use **Sign out of all sessions** to end it.

<DocsScreenshot
  name="settings-security-sign-out-device"
  alt="Sign out this session? confirmation dialog naming a device and showing the Sign out action"
/>

### Sign out of all sessions

1. In **Active sessions**, choose **Sign out of all sessions**.
2. Read the confirmation and choose **Sign out of all sessions**.

This signs out every device, including the current one. Unsaved work on those devices may be lost. It can take up to 30 minutes for sessions to end.

<DocsScreenshot
  name="settings-security-sign-out-all"
  alt="Sign out of all sessions confirmation warning that every device, including the current one, will be signed out"
/>

### Delete your account

1. Choose **Delete account**.
2. Enter the email address shown in the confirmation dialog exactly.
3. Choose **Delete account** to confirm.

Deletion permanently removes the account, Saved links, settings, Plugin Server connections, credentials, and active sessions. This cannot be undone. If you cancel the dialog, the account remains active.

<DocsScreenshot
  name="settings-security-delete-account"
  alt="Delete account dialog listing removed account data and asking you to type your email address to confirm"
/>

<DocSection id="faq">

## FAQ

<DocsFaq question="Why is my current device marked Current session?">
  It is the session you are using now. To end it along with the other sessions,
  choose **Sign out of all sessions**.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Can I undo account deletion?">
  No. Account deletion permanently removes the account and its listed data.
  There is no undo.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Why might another device stay signed in briefly?">
  It can take up to 30 minutes for sessions to end after **Sign out of all
  sessions**.
</DocsFaq>
