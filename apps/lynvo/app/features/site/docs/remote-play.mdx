<DocSection id="overview">

## Overview

Remote Play sends a Playable link from the current browser session to another connected Lynvo session. The receiving session opens it in its external Android player.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="configure">

## Configure

Open Lynvo and sign in on both devices with the same account. On the device that will receive the link, keep Lynvo open in its browser and make sure an Android player is installed.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="basics">

## Basics

### Connect to a device

1. On the sending device, choose **Remote Play**.
2. In **Connect Remote Play**, choose the receiving device from the device list.
3. If it is missing, keep Lynvo open on the receiver and choose **Search again**.

The sending session shows **Connected to** and the receiver shows **Controlled by** with the other device's name. **Cancel** closes the device picker.

### Send a Playable link

On the sending device, browse a Saved link and choose a Playable link. Lynvo sends its URL to the connected session, which opens it in the configured player. The receiving session displays a Remote Play overlay while it handles the command.

### Disconnect and reconnect

Disconnect Remote Play from the connected session when you are done. If a connection drops, open **Remote Play** again and choose the device. Keep Lynvo open on the receiving session so it can be found again.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="faq">

## FAQ

<DocsFaq question="Why isn’t my device in the list?">
  Both devices must be signed in to the same account, and Lynvo must remain open
  on the receiver. If the list is empty or could not load, choose **Search
  again**.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="What errors can Remote Play show?">
  The device list can say **Device list couldn’t be loaded** or **No Remote Play
  devices found**. Connection and delivery errors appear as **Remote Play
  couldn’t connect**, **Remote Play couldn’t disconnect**, **Couldn’t send the
  Remote Play command**, **Remote Play connection issue**, or **Remote Play
  request couldn’t be understood**. The receiver can show **Remote Play couldn’t
  open this link**. Check the connection, keep Lynvo open on both devices,
  reconnect, then try again. If the receiver cannot open the link, check that an
  Android player is installed and that the Playable link is still valid.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Does Remote Play stream video to my phone?">
  No. Remote Play sends the Playable link to another Lynvo session. The
  receiving device opens it in its external Android player.
</DocsFaq>

</DocSection>
