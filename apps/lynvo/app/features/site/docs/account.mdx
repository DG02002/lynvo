## Overview

Account settings show your Name and Email from Google. These fields are read-only in Lynvo.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="configure">

## Configure

Open [**Settings > Account**](/settings/account) to review the name and email address associated with your Google account.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="basics">

## Basics

### Review your account details

**Name** shows the name Lynvo receives from Google. If Google has not provided a name, Lynvo shows a dash. **Email** shows the address associated with your Google account. You cannot edit either value from this page.

For device sign-out or account deletion, open [**Settings > Security and sign-in**](/settings/security).

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="faq">

## FAQ

<DocsFaq question="Can I change my name or email in Lynvo?">
  No. Lynvo reads these values from your Google account. Update them with Google
  if they need to change.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Where do I sign out?">
Open [**Settings > Security and sign-in**](/settings/security) to sign out a session or delete the account.
</DocsFaq>
