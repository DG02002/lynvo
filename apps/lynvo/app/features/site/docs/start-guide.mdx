## Overview

Lynvo saves the Source URL you choose, finds playable links, and sends the link you select to an Android player. You bring the links and choose the player.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="configure">

## Configure

Set up your TV before saving links:

1. Install TV Bro on your Android TV.
2. Install Just (Video) Player or VLC on the TV.
3. Open Lynvo on a phone or computer and [sign in with Google](/docs/sign-in).
4. Open Lynvo in TV Bro and [sign in on the TV](/docs/android-tv).

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="basics">

## Basics

### Save a Source URL

1. Open Lynvo on the device where you have the URL.
2. Paste the URL into **Link** and choose **Save link**.
3. If Lynvo finds more than one playable choice, select the links you want and choose **Save**.

Read [how saving links works](/docs/saving-links) if you see an error, a loading state, or a link-selection dialog.

<DocsScreenshot
  name="start-guide-choose-links"

### Find the Saved link on your TV

Open Lynvo in TV Bro while signed in to the same account. Saved links sync across signed-in sessions. Choose the Saved link, browse its folders if needed, then open a Playable link in the configured player.

<DocsScreenshot
  name="start-guide-tv-library"
  alt="Lynvo Library on Android TV showing a Saved link and its Playable link choices"
/>

Read [how to open links](/docs/opening-links) for folder navigation and player choices.

### Send a link from your phone

Keep Lynvo open on the TV, then use [Remote Play](/docs/remote-play) from another signed-in device to choose the TV and send a Playable link to it.

</DocSection>

<DocSection id="faq">

## FAQ

<DocsFaq question="Does Lynvo provide videos to save?">
  No. Lynvo is a link library. You choose supported Source URLs and are
  responsible for having permission to access and play them.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Does Lynvo remember where I stopped watching?">
  No. Lynvo records whether you opened a link. The external player owns playback
  and its position.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="Can I use a different Android player?">
Lynvo supports Just (Video) Player, VLC, mpv, and MX Player. Choose defaults in [**Settings > Player**](/settings/player), or choose a player for one open from the Saved link menu.
</DocsFaq>

</DocSection>
