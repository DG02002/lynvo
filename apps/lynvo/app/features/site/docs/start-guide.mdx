## Get started

Use a URL you have permission to access. Lynvo keeps the links you choose; it does not provide videos or play them itself.

1. On your phone or computer, [sign in with Google](/docs/sign-in).
2. Open [Save](/save), paste a supported URL into **Link**, and choose **Save link**. If **Choose links** appears, select the Playable links you want and choose **Save**. The Saved link may show a loading state while Lynvo finds them. Read [Saving links](/docs/saving-links) if it needs attention.
3. On your Android TV, install [TV Bro](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phlox.tvwebbrowser) and [Just (Video) Player](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brouken.player) or [VLC for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.videolan.vlc).
4. Open Lynvo in TV Bro. Choose **Sign in**, then **Use a QR code**. Scan and approve the code with the phone or computer you signed in on. See [Android TV setup](/docs/android-tv) if you need help entering Lynvo's address.
5. In TV Bro, find the Saved link in your Library. Open it, browse any Folders, and choose a Playable link. Lynvo sends that link to the installed Android player.

Read [Opening links](/docs/opening-links) for folder navigation and player choices. You can also use [Remote Play](/docs/remote-play) to send a Playable link from your phone to an open TV session.

## FAQ

<DocsFaq question="Why can't I find my Saved link on the TV?">
  Check that Lynvo is signed in to the same account on both devices. If the
  Saved link is still loading or needs a selection, finish that step in your
  Library before opening its Playable link.
</DocsFaq>

<DocsFaq question="What if the link does not open in a player?">
  Install the player selected in [**Settings > Player**](/settings/player) on
  the TV. If Lynvo could not load the link, check its status in your Library
  and follow [Saving links](/docs/saving-links) to retry.
</DocsFaq>
